Photography Landscape 2025

Alpine Series

A long-form study of light and elevation across the Swiss and Italian Alps, shot over three winters. The project began as a personal inquiry into solitude and scale — how a human figure dissolves into ridgeline, how silence accumulates in snowfield.

Each image was made on medium format film, developed in-house and scanned at high resolution. The palette emerged from the material itself: the slight warmth of expired Kodak Portra, the grain that becomes texture at altitude.
