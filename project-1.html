Neon Stillness

Neon Stillness reimagined a boutique gallery as a twilight city fragment. The direction focused on controlled contrast: bright edge lighting, matte textures, and calm pacing that encouraged visitors to pause.

  • Role: Art Direction, Photography, Visual Identity
  • Timeline: 8 weeks
  • Deliverables: Mood film, gallery identity kit, launch visuals
